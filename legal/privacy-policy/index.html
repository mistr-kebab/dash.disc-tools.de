2. Hosting, CDN & Security

The Disc-Tools service - including the dashboard at dash.disc-tools.de and the website at disc-tools.de - is operated on server infrastructure hosted by Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland. Content delivery and DDoS protection are provided through Cloudflare. All connections are encrypted via TLS.

3. Server Logs

Our web server and API log each request including your IP address, timestamp, HTTP method, requested path, HTTP status code, user agent, and any error messages. These logs are automatically rotated and kept for a maximum of 30 days. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest in detecting and defending against abuse.

4. Cookies

We use the following strictly necessary cookies on the dashboard. They do not require consent under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR:

  • dash_token - JWT session token for authentication (7 days)
  • oauth_state - CSRF protection token during login (10 minutes)
  • cookie_consent - stores your cookie preferences (1 year)

No advertising, profiling, or third-party tracking cookies are used on the dashboard.

5. Analytics

We use a self-hosted instance of Umami Analytics on our own domain (umami.disc-tools.de) for website analytics. Umami is privacy-focused: it does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and all data is anonymized. No data is shared with third parties. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest in understanding how our services are used.

6. Discord OAuth2 Login

When you log in via Discord OAuth2, we receive your Discord user ID, username, avatar, email address, and guild memberships from the Discord API. We use this data exclusively to authenticate you and provide the dashboard. Your IP address is SHA-256 hashed with a per-service salt before storage - the raw IP is never persisted. We store this hashed IP for session tracking and abuse prevention. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR - performance of a contract (providing the authenticated dashboard service).

7. VPN & Proxy Detection

For non-authenticated dashboard visitors, we query proxycheck.io with your IP address to detect VPN, proxy, or hosting connections. Your IP is transmitted to proxycheck.io for this purpose. If a VPN/proxy is detected, access is restricted unless you log in. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest in protecting the service from automated abuse and scraping.

8. Username History

If you are in a Discord server where Disc-Tools is active, the bot may record changes to your username (old and new username, timestamp, and associated server ID). This feature can be disabled by the server owner. You can opt out of having your history publicly accessible via Profile Settings → Username History, which prevents anyone from looking up your past usernames. Stored usernames are retained until you request deletion.

9. Verification & Alt Detection

When you verify a Discord account through the bot, we store your Discord user ID, a SHA-256 hash of your IP address, and a verification timestamp. This data is used to detect alternate accounts (Premium feature). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the verification service, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for abuse detection.

10. Premium Subscriptions & Stripe

Premium payments are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, we transmit your Discord user ID to Stripe. Stripe returns a customer ID and subscription ID, which we store. The following data is stored in our database:

  • Discord user ID
  • Stripe customer and subscription IDs
  • Subscription status (active/inactive)
  • Start and expiry dates
  • Transaction history (type, duration, gift metadata)

Retention: Payment records are retained for the duration of the subscription plus 3 years after cancellation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with § 147 AO / § 257 HGB - statutory retention obligations under German tax and commercial law). In specific cases, data may be retained for up to 10 years for tax purposes. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to data they process independently.

11. GIF Uploads

When you upload a GIF via the bot, we store your Discord user ID, the GIF file on our server infrastructure, the original filename, a display name, optional tags, an NSFW flag, file dimensions, file size, view count, and moderation status. GIFs are publicly accessible by their assigned ID. Prohibited content (including CSAM, extreme violence, hate speech) is removed. We report CSAM to the relevant authorities. For age-restricted (NSFW) content, we require a date of birth for age verification. You may request deletion of your uploaded GIFs at any time by contacting us. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR - performance of a contract - and Art. 6(1)(c) for legal obligations regarding content moderation.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party except as necessary for service operation (see Sections 2, 3, 7, 10). Specifically:

  • Discord Inc. - OAuth2 authentication and bot API access
  • Stripe - payment processing
  • Cloudflare - CDN, DDoS protection, TLS termination
  • proxycheck.io - VPN/proxy detection (IP only, not linked to identity)
  • Hetzner - server hosting infrastructure

13. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR) - obtain confirmation and a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) - correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) - request deletion of your data
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR) - object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR) - where processing is based on consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, email info@disc-tools.de or contact us via the Support Server.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced via our Support Server. The current version is always available on this page.
